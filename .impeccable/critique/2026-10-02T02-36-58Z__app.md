## Priority Issues

1. **[P1] No focus indicator on form inputs and buttons.** Verified with real keyboard Tab on `/contact`: `name`, `email`, `message`, "Cart", and "Send message" compute identically at rest and focused (input border `1px solid #2A2A2A` both states; `box-shadow: none`; `outline-style: none`), while `:focus-visible` matches. Nav links do swap their bottom border to `#E8E8E8`. DESIGN.md specifies a `focus-ring` token (2px solid `#FFFFFF`, 2px offset) that is not implemented for these elements. WCAG 2.4.7.
2. **[P1] Cart error toast fires on every page load.** "Could not retrieve cart. Please try again." appears within 250 ms of `/product/sku-001` loading. No retry control, auto-dismisses. Reads as a broken store on first visit.
3. **[P1] Touch targets below the 24px WCAG minimum.** At 390×844, 8 of 14 interactive elements are under 44px in at least one dimension; footer "Story" is 37×17 and "Contact" is 52×17, header "Shop" 33×28, "Cart" 65×29. WCAG 2.5.8.
4. **[P1] Inter reaches the document root, against the design contract.** `getComputedStyle(document.documentElement).fontFamily` returns `Inter` on every local route; `app/assets/css/main.css:12` sets `--font-sans: 'Inter'` and Tailwind v4 preflight emits `html{font-family:var(--default-font-family)}`. Every rendered element still computes to JetBrains Mono, so nothing visibly renders in Inter — the defect is latent. Committed at HEAD, in the same commit that authored the DESIGN.md retiring Inter.
5. **[P2] Products have no visual identity.** Zero `<img>` elements site-wide. PDP gallery is a 485×607 `role="img"` SVG placeholder filled `#0E0E0E`. On mobile the three product cards stack full-width, putting roughly 1,250 px of empty near-black above the fold.
6. **[P2] Heading hierarchy skips levels.** Home is H1 → H3 with no H2; PDP has a single H1 and no subheads. `/contact` is the only correct route (H1 → H2).

## DESIGN.md Conformance

Verified compliant: zero border-radius on every rendered element; zero box-shadows; achromatic throughout with no retired token (#F7F3EC, #B08D57, #9F3A2F, #C0C0C0) present anywhere in the DOM or CSS custom properties; JetBrains Mono is the only computed font family on every rendered element; header, nav, main, footer landmarks all present; no horizontal scroll at 390px; contrast is strong throughout (minimum measured 7.46:1, maximum 17.14:1, comfortably past AAA for body text).

Verified non-compliant: the `focus-ring` token is not implemented on inputs and buttons (P1 above); Inter reaches `<html>` (P1 above).

## Strengths

1. **The copy is authored, not filler.** "Heavyweight cotton fleece, double-stitched.", "No cheap blanks. Just heavy black cotton and black thread embroidery.", "Black thread on black cotton. Design embroidered on chest panel; small brand mark on left sleeve." Every line describes a specific material and construction decision. This is the single strongest signal that the brand has a point of view.
2. **The restraint thesis is actually executed, not just declared.** Measured, not assumed: zero radius, zero shadow, one typeface, a nine-step achromatic ramp, and a 7.46:1 worst-case contrast floor. Most dark-mode e-commerce sites cannot hold this discipline; this one does.
3. **The PDP handles made-to-order honestly.** "MADE TO ORDER — COMING SOON" as a correctly disabled white CTA, with size selection and a five-panel accordion (FABRIC / EMBROIDERY / FIT / CARE / SHIPPING & RETURNS) above it. That is the right information architecture for a product that ships in 2–3 weeks.

## Minor Observations

- "BRAND" section on the homepage sets three centred paragraphs at mixed sizes (14px/14px/12px); centred multi-paragraph body copy is the weakest passage on the site.
- Hero tagline is set small and mono at 1440 but recentres on mobile — inconsistent alignment treatment across breakpoints.
- The home H1 is the header wordmark "STITCH & ASH" at 15px weight 400, with the hero title also reading "STITCH & ASH" at 40px; two H1s-worth of the same string at very different scales.
- A toast renders on load that DOM inspection shows contains no warm color; the red bar visible in the screenshot is a capture artifact, not a real token violation. Do not "fix" this.
